# After you click the Ideator link

You do **not** paste the link into a chat. You click it.

1. **Open the link** → Chrome (or your browser) opens.  
2. Choose **Open Grok Bot** (or **Download** if you don’t have the app yet).  
3. Tap **Add bot** / install Ideator when prompted.  
4. A starter message may appear automatically: *“Hi Ideator. Please write your memories.”*  
   - You didn’t type that — the app did. Leave it. Send/continue.  
5. Ideator should ask: **Where do your ideas show up first?**  
   - Pick the closest option, or type your own.  
6. Then: **When one is buzzing, what’s your real next step?**  
7. Then: paste **5–15 links/tabs** for a first win (cluster / mood-board read).  

**Skip anything** that says New Project, Repo, or coding workspace.

**You’re in Exploring** until you say an idea is locked.  
Later tools: **Forge** (form ideas), **Boot** (make it real), **Ass-kick** (drive toward a goal you name).
