# Ideator — 20-minute Grok Bot install script (for Kate)

**Operator:** Nick (you drive; she watches / clicks when you say)  
**Engine:** Grok Bot only  
**Do not:** create a Cursor project, Origin repo, or coding workspace  
**Prep:** Have `IDEATOR_AGENT_BRIEF.md` open on your machine; Kate on a video call with screen share

**Timing:** ~20 minutes. If anything asks for a “project/repo,” say skip and move on.

---

## Minute 0–2 — Frame (no clicks)

**Say:**
> We’re not setting up coding tools. We’re giving you one chat that thinks with you, remembers your ideas, and can nag you when something is ready to ship. One front door. That’s it.

**Anti-confusion clause (say twice if needed):**
> If anything says New Project, Repo, or Cursor project — skip it. That’s not Ideator.

---

## Minute 2–5 — Open Grok Bot / sign in

1. Open the Grok Bot app (or help her install/sign in with Cursor if needed).  
2. Confirm she sees the **sidebar** with assistants.  
3. Settings (if needed): sidebar account (bottom-left) or Cmd+, — only for sign-in.

**Checkpoint:** She can see a list of chats/agents. Stop. Don’t tour Settings.

---

## Minute 5–8 — Create the Ideator agent (front door)

**You do this** (or narrate while she clicks):

1. Create a new agent/teammate.  
2. **Name:** `Ideator` (or her preference: “Kate’s Ideator”).  
3. **Description / persona:** paste a short version, e.g.:

```text
You are Ideator — a thinking partner for people whose ideas are still forming.
Modes: Exploring (forge + keep threads) and Shipping (ass-kicker when locked).
Mirror ideas until they match; allow sideways reframes; lists are provisional mirrors with Other/reframe; never force A/B while the premise is soft.
Visual-friendly: prefer mood boards / clusters over walls of prose when useful.
Never start or push a coding project, Cursor repo, or Origin project unless she explicitly asks to build software.
First job: help her dump a mess of links or thoughts and mirror it back until it feels like hers.
```

4. Create. Open that agent’s chat — **this is the only front door**.

**Screenshot cue:** Sidebar with Ideator highlighted = “this is what you open forever.”

---

## Minute 8–10 — Seed the brief

1. In the Ideator chat, paste or attach `IDEATOR_AGENT_BRIEF.md` (or a 1-page cut).  
2. Say: “This is how you work with me — follow it.”  
3. Optional: click the agent name in the header for the info pane later; skip deep settings now.

**If the agent offers to scaffold a code project:** reply in chat: `No coding project. Stay in Ideator mode.`

---

## Minute 10–12 — How we talk (live demo)

**You type (or she does):**
> I’m testing how we’ll work. Mirror this: I have too many ideas in browser tabs and they never become real things.

**Agent should:** mirror, maybe a hybrid list, allow Other.  
**You show her:** pick closest *or* reframe — then the agent restates.  
**Say:** Lists are “so what you’re saying is…,” not final exams.

---

## Minute 12–16 — First win (links now; browser later)

**v0 (no browser connection required on call):**
1. She pastes **5–15 URLs** she’s been living in (or titles).  
2. Ask Ideator: cluster them, mood-board style, “what you seem to be circling,” three interpretations, cheapest next step.  
3. She corrects until it *matches*.

**Later (not blocking the call):** connect a **research-only browser** when your stack supports it. Privacy line:
> Only the browser we connect. Banking/email stay in a different browser.

**Screenshot cue:** Cluster / “what you’re circling” = the dopamine hit.

---

## Minute 16–18 — Modes in plain English

**Say / type:**
> Two modes. Exploring = forge and keep threads. Shipping = make me finish — only when I say this idea is locked.

Demo one line each:
- Exploring: “Keep this open; don’t turn it into tasks yet.”  
- Shipping: “Locked: ship a one-pager by Friday; don’t reopen premise without new evidence.”

She must **opt in** to Shipping per idea.

---

## Minute 18–20 — Leave-behind

Give her a 5-line cheat card:

1. Open **Ideator** only (sidebar).  
2. Dump mess / paste links.  
3. Correct the mirror until it matches.  
4. Exploring until you say locked → then Shipping.  
5. Skip Project/Repo forever unless you decide to build software.

**Success =** she can reopen Ideator tomorrow without you.

---
