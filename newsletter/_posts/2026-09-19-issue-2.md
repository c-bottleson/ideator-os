## Ways to work

Same three tools — one rename:

1. **Forge** — form the idea; mirror until it matches  
2. **Prototype** *(was Boot)* — make it real; first version; MVP = size of the first test  
3. **Ass-kick** — opt-in drive toward a named goal  

Portfolio still never ends in “Shipped.” Later is a shelf, not a grave.

---

## Pull doctrine (how Ideator asks)

Ideators often don’t know the answers yet — and they often know what *not* to do before what to do.

- One live question at a time  
- Offer a guess they can shove  
- Concrete scenes, not abstract fields (*noodle forever* vs *site by noon*)  
- “I don’t know yet” is first-class  
- Skip what isn’t ripe  
- **Default pace: unhurried** — pointed questions only when urgency shows  
- **Two nos → a provisional direction**

## Old idea, still true

Your best ideas don’t die from lack of brilliance. They die in tabs that never become a Prototype — and from quizzes that kill the buzz before the idea can breathe.

## Getting customers

Name the **first human conversation**, not a funnel.  
When do we talk to one real person, and what are we asking them?

If you can’t answer that yet — still Forging. Fine. Don’t Ass-kick a fog.
