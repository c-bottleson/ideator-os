## Ways to work

Ideator isn’t a notebook with vibes. It’s three tools:

1. **Forge** — form the idea; mirror until it matches; keep contradictions alive  
2. **Boot** — *make this real* — open a project space; ask what done/profit/learn means **in your words**; MVP ≠ maximum procrastination  
3. **Ass-kick** — opt-in relentless (shy of intolerable) drive toward that goal  

Portfolio stages: **Ideating → Building → Bettering → Maintaining → Later**  
There is no trophy shelf called “Shipped.” Finished-feeling work still lives in Bettering or Maintaining. What you’d call abandoned, we call **Later** — so it can come back without shame.

---

## Old idea, still true

Your best ideas don’t die from lack of brilliance. They die in tabs and boards that never become a Boot.

If your next step is always “more research,” you’re not lazy — you’re in a loop. Boot is how you break it. Ass-kick is how you don’t wander back into the loop.

---

## Getting customers (ideators’ biggest pain)

One move this week — not a funnel course:

**Write the sentence a buyer would say out loud when the pain hits.**  
Not your product name. Their words. Put it at the top of `GOAL.md` when you Boot.

If you can’t write that sentence, you’re still Forging. That’s fine. Don’t Ass-kick a premise that isn’t real yet.

---

## Finding doers

Ideators need people who build. Before you hunt:

Boot one idea far enough that a doer could open the folder and know the goal.  
A doer packet is: goal in their words + links/mood + “done looks like X” + what you will still own.

No packet → you’re hiring someone to mind-read.
